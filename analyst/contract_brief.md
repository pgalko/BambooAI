## The brief

The person framed this question before the run, and BRIEF holds what was agreed.

- What the answer is for: the report's opening paragraph says what the result means for it.
- What is fixed before the run - the comparison, the measure, the period - is what you compute. A part the brief
  does not fix is yours to choose.
- What it rests on is the person's frame, not evidence: a line marked "does not hold" or "cannot be checked" is
  not relied on, and a line from an earlier result is a claim to test.
- Every line under Also check is tested.
- A line under Saved analyses is an earlier analysis with the passages the person chose from it. `SHOW SAVED <k>`
  opens it in your next prompt: its report and the code of its cells. Its method may carry over: write it anew,
  its names are not in your kernel. Its numbers are history about the data it ran on - another person's where the
  line says so - and no result of this run.
- Where the data cannot give what is fixed - the measure is not recorded, the period is not covered, a side of
  the comparison is empty - put nothing else in its place: ASK, saying what is missing and what the data would
  allow.
