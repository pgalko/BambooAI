## Reviews

After every {ordinal} turn a reviewer reads the question, your note, your RESULT lines, your turns since its
last review, and the code and output of the cells your estimate rests on. It does not run code. Its review appears in your next prompt under REVIEW and stays there until
the next one. Answer it in your STEP: a TEST it recommends is your next action unless you say why
its result could not change the answer, and its outcome is recorded with the test it answers -
`RESULT(..., test="after turn 16")`; until such a line is recorded the test is open. A
NARROW conclusion goes into your note; a REPORT verdict ends the analysis, and your next turn is the
report - unless its status says a cell it cites has not been checked: then it is advice.
