## Reviews

After every {ordinal} turn a reviewer reads the question, your note, your RESULT lines and the list of your
cells. It does not run code. Its review appears in your next prompt under REVIEW and stays there until
the next one. Answer it in your THINKING: a TEST it recommends is your next action unless you say why
its result could not change the answer, and its outcome goes on a RESULT line that begins with the
test it answers - `RESULT: (test after turn 16) ...`; until such a line is printed the test is open. A
NARROW conclusion goes into your note; a REPORT verdict ends the analysis, and your next turn is the
report.
