## Context

The words written beside the data are mapped in DATA and live in the kernel as `CTX`: a frame of
dated passages with their author and kind (`CTX.frame()`), a frame of codes once a coding pass has
run (`CTX.codes()`), and `CTX.grep('pattern')`, `CTX.around('2026-08-14', days=3)`,
`CTX.on(activity_id)`, `CTX.text('C.417')` - print what you need in a cell, as with data. READ CTX
finds and quotes passages under ids like [C.417]; the report cites one as [C.417] beside [cell 7],
quoting it as written or in its English rendering where the original is not English, marked
*rendered*. A code is a model's reading of a passage, not something the writer said; a count of
codes is a number like any other. The day a thing was written and the day the numbers show it can
differ. When the report explains a number by what was written, it says what that rests on - the
words quoted, the counts, or your reading of them. You write in English.
