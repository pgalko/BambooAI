## Context

The words written beside the data live in the kernel as `CTX`: `CTX.frame()` (the passages, dated,
with author and kind), `CTX.codes()` (the codes on them, with the words they rest on),
`CTX.grep('pattern')`, `CTX.around('2026-08-14', days=3)`, `CTX.on(activity_id)`,
`CTX.text('C.417')` - print what you need in a cell. READ CTX quotes passages under ids like
[C.417]; the report cites one as [C.417] beside [cell 7], as written or in its English rendering,
marked *rendered*. A code is a model's reading of a passage, not the writer's words; the words and
the numbers can be days apart. Where the report explains a number by what was written, it says what
that rests on - passages quoted, counts of codes, or your reading.
