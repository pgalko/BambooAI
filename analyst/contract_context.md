## Context

The words written beside the data live in the kernel as `CTX`: `CTX.frame()` (the passages, dated,
with author and kind), `CTX.codes()` (the codes on them, with the words they rest on, which way each
points against the person's usual state, said or inferred, and the days it is about), `CTX.days()`
(the codes on the days they are about, counted by direction), `CTX.episodes()` (runs of days a code
points worse); `CTX.grep('pattern')`, `CTX.around('2026-08-14', days=3)` and `CTX.on(activity_id)`
return text to print (rows with `as_frame=True`), `CTX.text('C.417')` one passage whole. READ CTX
quotes passages under ids like [C.417]; the report cites one as [C.417] beside [cell 7], as written
or in its English rendering, marked *rendered*. A code is a model's reading of a passage, not the
writer's words; the words and the numbers can be days apart. Where the report explains a number by
what was written, it says what that rests on - passages quoted, counts of codes, or your reading.
