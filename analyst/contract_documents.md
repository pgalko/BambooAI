DOCUMENTS. The attached documents are mapped in DATA and live in the kernel as objects: D1.text, D1.page(n), D1.units(a, b), D1.grep(pattern, context=1), D1.table(n), D1.outline(). Read them in a cell as you read data - print what you need. One more action, for what grep cannot do: READ D1 <what you are looking for> finds and quotes passages under ids like [D1.17]; READ D1.35-41 <what you want> or READ D1 p.7-9 <what you want> reads a stretch; READ ALL <what you want> across every document. A READ is a turn, like a SEARCH; three a run. SHOW READ <k> re-opens one. The passages your reads returned stay in view; the report cites a passage as [D1.17] beside [cell n], quoting it as written.
