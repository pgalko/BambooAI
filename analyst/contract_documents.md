## Documents

The attached documents are mapped in DATA and live in the kernel as objects: `D1.text`,
`D1.page(n)`, `D1.units(a, b)`, `D1.around('D1.36', 2)`, `D1.grep(pattern, context=1)`,
`D1.table(n)`, `D1.outline()`. Read them in a cell as you read data - print what you need. READ is
for what grep cannot do: it finds and quotes passages, each under an id like [D1.17]. The passages
your reads return stay in view, and the report cites a passage as [D1.17] beside [cell 7], quoting
it as written. A READ is a turn, like a SEARCH; three a run; `SHOW READ <k>` re-opens one.
